The TARS approach

What we do — and how we do it.

We help you turn uncertainty into a set of practical next steps. TARS is designed to support your decisions, not make them for you.

01

Understand you

We ask about your interests, strengths, preferences, current education and the kind of work you want to explore.

02

Find the gap

For engineering students, compare the curriculum you upload with skills commonly requested in the target role. We highlight possible gaps to investigate.

03

Build a roadmap

Get practical suggestions for skills, projects, internships, communication, group discussions and interviews — without an exam timetable.

04

Prepare for Selection

Practise group discussions, aptitude tests, HR interviews and technical interviews. Build communication skills, improve confidence and prepare for company selection rounds.

Transparency: this version performs a local, keyword-based curriculum comparison. It does not send your details or PDF to ChatGPT, and it does not have live access to company hiring databases. Job requirements change, so verify them using official company career pages.
